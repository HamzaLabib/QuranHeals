Listening: how much you listen is your decision: no minimum playback is required, and the page records none. Every confirmation is your judgement. Bulk: Accept All Proposed, Reject All Proposed and Accept All Edited (top of the ayah list) show a preview first and never submit.
Mouse: click the waveform to move the playhead · double-click to place the selected ayah's start there · drag a marker to move it · Shift-drag to select a time range (for a flag) · wheel to zoom, Shift+wheel to scroll · click the overview strip to jump.
Where to place a start: at the onset of the ayah's first sound (the first letter, or the breath right before it), not in the middle of the preceding silence. Seeking to an ayah must begin its recitation promptly.
