Ayah timing review

Open the folder made by review/make-packages.mjs. The list is ordered by risk: listen from the top.

How it works

1. Open the packages folder. 2. Pick a surah. 3. For each boundary on its worklist, press ▶ Transition, listen, move the boundary if it is off, then mark it Correct, Needs correction or Unresolved. 4. When every worklist boundary is Correct, press Approve whole surah and Export review.

Keys: Space play / pause · T transition of the selected boundary · C correct · N needs correction · U unresolved · J/K previous / next boundary · [/] move it 50 ms
