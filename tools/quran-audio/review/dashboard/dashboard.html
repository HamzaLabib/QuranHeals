How to review

  1. Type your name (top). Use the same spelling every time; another person must use another name.
  2. Pick a recording (Start review) or a start from Needs attention. The review page opens; choose Start as Reviewer 1 (or 2, if someone else already started it).
  3. Listen from the beginning to the end. For each ayah, select it and either:
    • Confirm proposed (C), only after hearing that the dashed automatic proposal is at the onset of the ayah's first sound; or
    • put the playhead at the onset and press M, refine with [ ], check with Q (before) / W (after), then judge it 1 confirmed, 2 uncertain or 3 rejected.
    Muhammad Rifaat recordings have no proposal: every start is placed by ear, with the Phase 4.5 evidence shown only as places to listen.
  4. Place the recitation end (E), answer the whole-recording checks, flag repetitions, missing passages and extra audio, then Submit.
  5. Reviewer 2 does the same independently: Reviewer 1's placements stay hidden until both have submitted. Disagreements over 250 ms are then resolved by one proposing a time and the other accepting it.

Progress is saved on every change (and on S). Closing the browser or restarting the computer loses nothing. Backups are made automatically.

Statuses are kept apart: alignment completed · human review pending · first review completed · second review completed · technical verification completed · rights pending · approved for development · active in development. A technically verified recording is not a permission: rights stay pending until the owner records a decision, and activation is a separate owner step.
