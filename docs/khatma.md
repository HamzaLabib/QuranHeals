# أورادي وختماتي: Wird progress and Khatmas

**Status (2026-10-13):** implemented on the device; a signed-in account's أوراد and Khatmas also sync across its devices (`docs/auth-and-sync/reading-sync.md`). Code: `mobile/src/mushaf/khatmas.ts`, `readingStops.ts`, `ramadan.ts`; screens `app/my-reading.tsx`, `app/khatma-complete.tsx`; the reader `app/(tabs)/mushaf.tsx`.

## The rules

- **Each ورد is independent.** Every reading stop keeps its own saved page, progress, current reading cycle and completed Khatmas. There is no limit on the number of ورد; a 500-stop storage safeguard remains.
- **Progress is the saved page out of 604.** It is a position, not proof that every page was recited: nothing records pages as read (a save from page 1 to 604 marks pages 2–603 as nothing), and it is shown as «الموضع ٥٢٪» / "Position 52%", with a note that the data stays on this device and a Khatma counts only when confirmed. Declaring a Khatma completed outside the app stays possible: save the ورد at 604 and confirm, or use the explicit choice on a ورد at 604. It is shown as a whole percent, rounded down, so it is 100% only on page 604. It changes only through an explicit save:
  - the reader's bookmark;
  - creating a ورد with a starting page;
  - a confirmation (back to page 1) or an undo (back to 604).

  Opening, browsing, turning to or searching for a page never changes it.
- **Eligibility.** A ورد becomes eligible for a Khatma only when a save takes it from an earlier page to page 604 (status `pending`).
- **Confirmation is always manual.** It is offered in three places:
  - **The reader:** right after that save, it shows «ما شاء الله، أتممت ختم القرآن» with **إتمام الختمة** and **ليس الآن**.
  - **أورادي وختماتي:** the ورد's card shows **تأكيد إتمام الختمة**.
  - **The du'a screen:** when opened for that ورد (`/khatm-dua?wird=<id>`), it offers a separate **تأكيد إتمام الختمة** button. Reading the du'a never counts anything.

  If the user chooses **ليس الآن**, the completion stays pending, the page stays saved, and the reader does not prompt again.
- **Confirming:**
  1. Creates one record: the ورد's id and name, the local completion date, and the Ramadan classification.
  2. Starts the next cycle at page 1.
  3. Opens تقبل الله ختمتك, which offers the optional du'a and «العودة إلى ختماتي».

  The record's id comes from the cycle's id, so a double tap, a retry, a stale screen or a crash between writes can never count a cycle twice. The UI also disables the button while a confirmation runs.
- **Ambiguous stops at 604.** This covers stops saved at 604 before this feature, and stops created directly on 604. They are shown as "Saved at page 604", with nothing counted and nothing discarded. A separate, explicitly worded choice ("I completed a Khatma with this Wird" → "Only if you have read the whole Quran with …") records one.
- **Correcting a mistake.** **Remove** on a history record, after a confirmation, deletes that record; every count is recalculated from the records.
  - If that confirmation started the ورد's current cycle and the ورد has not been saved since, the ورد returns to page 604 with the completion pending again.
  - Otherwise its current progress is kept.
  - Other ورد are never touched.
- **Rename** keeps the ورد's cycle and count. **Delete**, after confirmation, removes the ورد and its cycle. Its completed Khatmas stay in the history and totals under the name they were recorded with.
- **Statistics (two counters, side by side):** **الختمات / Khatmas** counts every confirmed Khatma, Ramadan ones included, and **ختمات رمضان / Ramadan Khatmas** counts only those completed in Ramadan. Both are always computed from the confirmed records, never kept as separate counters. The non-Ramadan count is no longer shown, but every record keeps its classification (`total = ramadan + nonRamadan` still holds in `khatmaStats`).

## Ramadan classification

- **Date:** the device's **local civil date** at the moment of confirmation (midnight to midnight in its time zone), never UTC. The ورد's name and its creation date are never used.
- **Calendar:** **Umm al-Qura** (the Saudi official calendar, ICU's `islamic-umalqura`), from a fixed table of Ramadan's first and last civil days for 1400–1500 AH (1980–2077). The table is generated by `mobile/scripts/generate-ramadan-table.mjs`, written to `src/mushaf/generated/ummAlQuraRamadan.ts`, and checked against ICU for every day by `tests/ramadan.test.ts`.
  - Every device classifies the same date the same way, whatever its Intl support. Hermes on iOS and Android differ in their calendar support.
  - The Islamic day starting at sunset is not used. Local moon sighting can differ by a day at either end of Ramadan.
  - A date outside the table is classified as outside Ramadan.

## Storage and accounts

Storage is per owner, like favorites and reflections (`storage/localDataOwner.ts`). The guest uses the keys below; each account that signed in on this device has `<key>:account:<userId>`.

| Key | Contents |
|---|---|
| `quran-heals:mushaf-reading-stops:v1` | The stops: `{ id, name, page, updatedAt }`, unchanged from before. Existing stops stay guest data. |
| `quran-heals:mushaf-active-reading-stop:v1` | The active ورد's id. |
| `quran-heals:mushaf-khatmas:v1` | `{ schema: 1, cycles: { [stopId]: cycle }, records: [...] }`. |

- **No migration.** The stops' format did not change. A stop with no cycle on record gets a stable first cycle (`first-<stopId>`) when it is read.
- **Interrupted writes.** Every change runs one at a time (`withReadingLock`). A confirmation or undo writes the record and new cycle first, with a `pendingPage` marker, then the stop's page, then clears the marker. A load after an interruption finishes it. A save made by an interrupted write is caught up from the stop's page.
- **Guest and accounts.**
  - Sign-in shows the existing "Add to this account" question when the guest has ورد or Khatmas. "Add to this account" merges them: a ورد whose name the account already uses gets a numbered name, and records are kept by id.
  - Sign-out shows the guest's own data again.
  - Account deletion removes that account's ورد, Khatmas and recent emotions from this device.
- **Synced for signed-in accounts** (account-based like favorites, no Sync Password; a guest's stay on the device): `docs/auth-and-sync/reading-sync.md`. Sync never confirms a Khatma, and counts are always computed from the records on each device.

## Home and navigation

- **Bottom tabs:** Home, Mushaf, أورادي وختماتي (Recitations & Khatmas, `/my-reading`), Reflections (`app/(tabs)/`). Home's header has Favorites and Settings; the Settings gear (`components/SettingsHeaderButton.tsx`) is also in the header of Favorites, Reflections, All Emotions, every ayah screen, أورادي وختماتي, Credits and the Khatma completion screen (alone at its header's far end; it has no Back button), never on the Mushaf reader, Settings itself or دعاء ختم القرآن. Home's متابعة التلاوة / Continue Reciting card has one action, المصحف (since 2026-10-14). أورادي وختماتي opens from its tab and from the last row of the reader's ورد list (which switches to its tab).
- **No saved position:** the Mushaf opens page 1 (the active ورد's page, else the most recently saved ورد's, when there is one). Page 61 stays special only because it is bundled.
- **Wording:** English says "Recitations" (My Recitations & Khatmas, Continue Reciting); routes, storage keys and identifiers keep their names (`/my-reading`, `myReading`, `quran-heals:mushaf-reading-stops:v1`).
- **Shared state:** Home, the reader and أورادي وختماتي read the same storage and reload on every change (`subscribeToReadingData`) and on every account change.
- **Mushaf actions:** Home's المصحف and a card's Continue reading make that ورد active and open its saved page, even when the reader is already open (`/mushaf?page=N&at=…`). Tapping the Mushaf tab returns to the page the reader was on. The Mushaf has no tab bar (Normal Mode or full screen): its Back arrow, or Android Back, returns to the tab before it (Home when opened directly), where the four tabs show again.
