# Delete your Quran Heals account

Quran Heals works without an account. If you signed in with Apple or Google to sync between devices, you can permanently delete that account and all of its synced data at any time.

## Delete in the app (fastest)

1. Open Quran Heals and make sure you are signed in.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Danger Zone → Delete Account**.
3. Type **DELETE** to confirm and tap **Delete my account**.
4. If asked, sign in again with the same Apple or Google account. This confirms it is really you.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

## What is deleted

From our servers, immediately:

- your account (sign-in provider, provider account identifier, email address);
- all sign-in sessions;
- synced favorites and preferences;
- synced reflections (which were end-to-end encrypted) and your encrypted reflection key;
- for Apple accounts, the stored Apple credential; we also revoke Quran Heals' access to your Apple ID.

On the device you deleted from, that account's reflections and favorites are also removed.

## What is not deleted

- **Copies on your other devices.** Other devices you signed in on are signed out but keep any reflections and favorites already stored on them. To remove them, uninstall the app on that device (or clear the app's data).
- **Data you used without an account** (guest data) on any device. Uninstall the app to remove it.
- **App settings** on the device (language, appearance, text size).
- **Issue reports** you sent. They are not linked to your account. To have one deleted, email us (below) with the email address or details you included in it.
- **Backups.** Deleted data may remain in our database provider's encrypted backups for up to [TO CONFIRM: period] before being overwritten.

- **Apple:** Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → Quran Heals → Stop Using Apple ID.
- **Google:** myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → Quran Heals → Delete all connections.

Removing access this way does not delete the data on our servers; use the app or email us for that.
